# Farm Fun First Look

Design the first visual concept for the new Udderly Ridiculous Farm Life homepage.

This is a VISUAL DESIGN EXPLORATION only.

Do not build a backend, CMS, authentication, booking integration, database, or complicated functionality.

Use placeholder content and placeholder farm photography for now.

The purpose of this first version is to establish:

- overall visual personality

- colour usage

- typography

- header/navigation

- buttons

- cards

- photography treatment

- spacing

- playful graphic language

- responsive/mobile direction

BRAND

Business:

Udderly Ridiculous Farm Life

This is an agritourism farm in Ontario with experiences involving Mini Highland cattle, alpacas, goats, miniature donkeys and other farm animals.

The brand is deliberately RIDICULOUS.

It is:

- fun

- quirky

- unexpected

- cheeky

- energetic

- warm

- highly photographic

- full of personality

It is NOT:

- minimalist tech

- corporate SaaS

- sterile

- beige luxury

- generic rustic farmhouse

- overly elegant

- childish preschool design

- cowboy / western themed

- full of cliché barn icons

Think:

"A really good designer was allowed to have fun."

The website should still feel polished, trustworthy and professionally designed, but it should have obvious personality.

VISUAL PRINCIPLES

Do not create a simplistic flat tech website.

Use:

- playful asymmetry

- overlapping photography where appropriate

- slightly unexpected card arrangements

- organic shapes

- graphic cutouts

- sticker-like accent elements

- oversized type

- occasional rotated or offset elements

- playful arrows / doodle-like accents

- strong photography

- varied section compositions

The site should feel alive.

Avoid making every section:

heading

paragraph

3 identical cards

button

Mix layouts and create rhythm.

It can be weird in controlled ways.

COLOUR SYSTEM

Use these colours exactly.

CTA BUTTONS ONLY:

#c7579a

CTA button default:

- background #c7579a

- white text

CTA hover:

- background remains #c7579a

- text changes from white to #3ed0e4

Do not use #c7579a as a general background colour. Reserve it primarily for clickable CTA buttons.

PRIMARY ACCENT:

#d10373

SECONDARY ACCENT:

#3ed0e4

WHITE BACKGROUND:

#ffffff

BEIGE BACKGROUND:

#fff8e6

BLUE BACKGROUND:

#98d3dd

HEADLINE / TITLE COLOUR:

#613314

Body text can use a dark neutral/brown that works naturally with #613314.

Use ALL of the listed brand colours somewhere in this first homepage concept so we can evaluate how they work together.

Do not use gradients in this first pass.

TYPOGRAPHY

The actual brand font is Trade Gothic.

Use the closest practical web-safe/free approximation available.

Preferred heading direction:

Barlow Condensed

Use it boldly and prominently for major titles and strong display text.

Suggested system:

Primary/display:

Barlow Condensed

- bold / extra bold

- uppercase selectively

- oversized headings

- compressed energetic feeling

Secondary/body:

DM Sans

- easy to read

- friendly

- modern without feeling technical

Tertiary/accent:

Fraunces

- use VERY sparingly

- short quotes

- funny editorial notes

- little unexpected phrases

- never as the main body font

Do not overuse the tertiary font.

PHOTOGRAPHY

The design should be photography-led.

For placeholders, use large farm-animal photography featuring things like:

- fluffy Mini Highland cows

- alpacas

- goats

- miniature donkeys

- people interacting with animals

- candid farm moments

Avoid generic landscape photography dominating the site.

The animals are the celebrities.

Photography can:

- break out of containers

- overlap colour blocks

- use unusual crops

- appear in playful frames

- occasionally be slightly rotated

Keep it polished rather than scrapbook-chaotic.

BUILD ONLY THIS FIRST HOMEPAGE CONCEPT

White background.

Logo placeholder on left:

Udderly Ridiculous Farm Life

Navigation:

Experiences

Meet the Herd

Stay

Our Farm

CTA on right:

Book an Experience

Use the CTA button colour #c7579a.

The header should already feel like the brand, not a generic SaaS nav.

Desktop and mobile should both be considered.

2. HERO

Use a strong, highly photographic hero.

Possible placeholder headline:

GET OUT HERE.

IT'S RIDICULOUS.

or:

COME DO SOMETHING

UDDERLY RIDICULOUS.

Use #613314 prominently for the headline.

Include placeholder supporting copy explaining that visitors can meet Highland cows, alpacas, goats, donkeys and other animals through immersive farm experiences.

Primary CTA:

Book an Experience

Secondary text/link:

See what the heck we do

Use beige #fff8e6 somewhere prominently in the hero composition.

Also incorporate one of the brighter accent colours through graphic shapes, labels, doodles or small elements.

The hero should immediately communicate:

animals

fun

professionalism

ridiculousness

Do not make it a normal centered hero with text over a full-width stock image.

Create an interesting composition.

3. FEATURED EXPERIENCES

Use #98d3dd as the main section background.

Heading placeholder:

PICK YOUR

RIDICULOUSNESS.

Show 3 or 4 experience cards, for example:

Mini Highland Cow Experience

Goat Cuddles

Alpaca Walk & Bond

Mini Donkey Picnic

Each card should have:

- prominent photography

- title

- small placeholder fact such as duration or season

- short copy

- clear interaction

Cards do not all need to be identical rectangles.

Experiment with image crop, slight offsets, playful labels, or overlapping accents.

Use #d10373 and #3ed0e4 for graphical accents.

Keep CTA buttons #c7579a only.

Background:

Use a mixture of:

#613314

#d10373

#3ed0e4

Animal cards can feel more editorial and personality-driven than normal product cards.

5. LARGE BRAND / CTA SECTION

Use #98d3dd as a strong background or create a composition using blue + white + accent elements.

Large playful statement such as:

YOU COULD HAVE

A NORMAL WEEKEND.

BUT WHY?

Include one strong CTA:

Find an Experience

Use #c7579a button with white text.

Hover text becomes #3ed0e4.

Include a large animal image breaking out of the section composition.

This section should feel memorable and slightly ridiculous.

6. FOOTER

Keep it functional but still branded.

Include:

Experiences

Meet the Herd

Stay

Adopt

Corporate

Our Farm

Contact

Include placeholder social icons.

Include a small playful line such as:

Made with questionable judgement and excellent animal care.

Use the beige, blue, white or brown palette rather than creating a generic black footer.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Design desktop and mobile intentionally.

On mobile:

- preserve personality

- keep oversized typography where possible

- avoid collapsing everything into boring stacked cards

- make buttons comfortably tappable

- photography should remain prominent

- overlapping elements should not create horizontal scrolling

ACCESSIBILITY

Maintain readable contrast.

Do not make important text tiny.

Buttons and links must remain obvious.

Animations, if included, should be subtle and not necessary to understand the interface.

IMPORTANT

Do not over-polish the content or create lots of additional homepage sections.

This is a first visual-language prototype.

I want to judge:

- colours

- typography

- composition

- personality

- buttons

- photography

- cards

- overall vibe

Build the header, hero, featured experiences, Meet the Herd teaser, one large CTA section, and footer only.
